## 6. Caveats

1. **`detect_action_clips.py` mislabels sitting.** It has no `sitting` label, and its
   `airborne_frac` feature fires on seated frames (it infers "feet unloaded" from pelvis
   height) — the long sits come back labelled `jumping` with `air%` up to **97.1**. Its
   output in `data_analysis/pico_20260925/` is **not** usable for this session; use §3/§4.
2. **`joint_pos` legs are identically zero** (upper-body-only retargeting). Any leg analysis
   must go through `smpl_pose` / `smpl_joints`.
3. **Leg-raise detection is root-relative**, so a raise is not distinguishable from crossing
   the ankles or from pelvis tilt. The 0.30 m cases are unambiguous; the 0.12–0.14 m ones
   should be eyeballed before use.
4. **72 Hz source rate** (~20% dropped) in both sessions, plus ~24 s of stall in `183646`.
5. **Sim, not hardware** — torque/saturation figures in the paired logs are simulator
   outputs, not motor telemetry.
6. **No reference trajectory exists for these motions**, so `mpjpe_l`-style tracking metrics
   are undefined; only control-loop and robustness metrics apply to the paired runs.
